Formula reference

u is time within the beat (0 ≤ u < T). P Q R S W are repeating unit-height Gaussian pulses; W is the T-wave pulse. Use * to multiply and ^ for powers.

sin cos tan exp ln log sqrt abs tanh min max gauss

gauss(t,c,w) = exp(-0.5*((t-c)/w)^2). Center c and width w are seconds.

Example: y + 0.1*sin(2*pi*2*t). Formulas replace the lead function; y always means its unmodified model, so edits do not stack.

Discontinuous expressions or corners have undefined derivatives at their discontinuities. Wraps of u can introduce jumps.

The waveform is the equation

The generator sums smooth repeating Gaussian components: f(t) = Σ Aⱼ exp[−(t−μⱼ−kT)²/(2σⱼ²)]. Derivatives follow the chain rule; integration gives signed voltage–time area. The QRS width control changes Gaussian widths and spacing, not an automatically measured clinical duration.

This is an original, illustrative signal model, not a patient-specific electrophysiology simulation or an implementation of ECGSYN. Chest, right-sided, and posterior leads have individually chosen illustrative morphologies; extra leads are synthetic functions, not reconstructions from a standard 12-lead ECG.

Lead relationships

In linked mode: III = II − I; aVR = −(I + II)/2; aVL = I − II/2; aVF = II − I/2. Independently editing a derived limb lead requires switching linked mode off. Re-enabling it suspends those individual overrides.

The paired layout groups your leads spatially. Placement opposite another trace does not guarantee a reciprocal clinical finding.

Scale & precision

Grid cells represent 1 mm at the chosen speed and gain. Screen pixels and the fitted print layout do not guarantee physical millimeters. Use the digital calipers or CSV coordinates for measurements. The printout is a visual reference, not ruler-calibrated paper.

CSV contains all selected simultaneous signals sampled at 1,000 Hz. Waveform pixels are a display approximation. Derivatives may be undefined at corners or discontinuities; Simpson integration is numerical, with a target absolute tolerance of 10⁻⁷ mV·s for smooth expressions.
